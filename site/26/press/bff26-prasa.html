Bitcoin FilmFest · Warsaw · updated 2026-06-19

📝 Summary in brief (free to use)

The fourth edition of Bitcoin FilmFest took place on June 4–7, 2026 at Warsaw's Kinoteka, inside the Palace of Culture and Science. It is the world's largest film festival devoted to Bitcoin culture — not an industry conference but a cultural event built around cinema, art and community. This year's edition was co-located with MoneroKon, a conference on privacy and financial technologies; one ticket gave access to both events.

The festival drew more than 400 people in total from 15 countries. The audience watched 16 finished films and 8 productions in progress, plus panels, workshops, concerts and social events. The German dark comedy Bitcoin Castle won two Golden Rabbits — the jury award and the audience award. Best Short went to the animated MaxisClub. In the AI film contest "Future of Money, Money of the Future", the main prize of 1.5 million satoshi went to a UK creator working under the name Naritamoto.

From the stage came an announcement: the fifth edition of Bitcoin FilmFest will take place in Warsaw on June 24–27, 2027. The festival was founded in Poland in 2022 and each year brings an international community of Bitcoin-culture creators and audiences to Warsaw.
