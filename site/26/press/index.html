Bitcoin FilmFest 2026 · June 4–7 · Kinoteka, Warsaw

Bitcoin FilmFest is a festival of Bitcoin culture built around cinema. The films are the spine; around them sit panels, workshops, live music, art and a community stage. The fourth edition runs June 4–7, 2026 at Kinoteka, inside Warsaw's Palace of Culture, and this year shares its dates and venue with MoneroKon — one ticket covers both. Last year drew 200-plus guests from more than 15 countries.

When
June 4–7, 2026
Where
Kinoteka, Palace of Culture, Warsaw
Edition
4th — co-located with MoneroKon
Ticket
€120 Full Pass (co-valid for MoneroKon, includes a Trezor Model T) · €39 LITE one-day pass · paid in Bitcoin
Programme
21 films (18 cinema + 3 VOD-only) · panels · workshops · music
New in 2026
Future of Money AI contest — 2.5M sat prize
