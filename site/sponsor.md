    <h1>Become a Friend</h1>
    <p>Bitcoin FilmFest is a grassroots project — every edition runs on the people, studios, and companies who back Bitcoin culture with us. Becoming a Friend of BFF is how you show up on that list.</p>
  </header>

  <div class="contribute-copy">
    <p>"Friends of BFF" is our name for sponsors and partners: the exchanges, hardware makers, studios, associations, and Bitcoin businesses whose logos appear on the festival edition pages and, where relevant, on-site during the event.</p>
    <p>There is no fixed price list — support has ranged from cash sponsorship to venue, travel, prize, and in-kind contributions (equipment, hosting, printing, tickets, and more). We work out the right fit with each Friend rather than sell a rigid package.</p>
  </div>

  <ul class="contribute-roles">
    <li>Featured Friend — top-row, larger logo placement across the edition page and event materials, for our lead partners and sponsors.</li>
    <li>Friend — standard logo placement in the Friends row on the edition page.</li>
    <li>In-kind partner — equipment, venue, travel, prizes, or services provided instead of cash, credited the same way.</li>
  </ul>
