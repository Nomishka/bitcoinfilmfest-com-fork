## Overview

**Menger. Notes on the Margin** is a Polish documentary that honors the 150th anniversary of Carl Menger's *Principles of Economics* (1874), the foundational work that initiated the Austrian School of Economics. Director Tomasz Agencki interweaves two narrative threads: a historical biographical account of Menger's life, intellectual development, and influence on economic thought, alongside a contemporary story of economists and thinkers rediscovering and applying Menger's ideas in the 21st century.

## Significance for Bitcoin Culture

Carl Menger's revolutionary theory of subjective value and marginal utility provided the philosophical scaffolding that later Austrian economists — including Ludwig von Mises and Friedrich Hayek — would use to critique fiat currency and central banking. Menger's emphasis on individual choice, spontaneous order, and skepticism of state interference directly parallels Bitcoin's core principles of decentralized money, individual sovereignty, and peer-to-peer exchange. The film articulates this legacy clearly, positioning Menger as an intellectual ancestor to cryptocurrency and the modern sovereign-money movement.

## Production & Release

The film was shot across four countries — Austria, Czech Republic, Poland, and France — to trace Menger's intellectual journey and the geographic centers of Austrian economic thought. It premiered in December 2023 at the Atlantic cinema in Warsaw, followed by its English-language premiere at Bitcoin FilmFest 2024 (Kinoteka, Warsaw) on April 19, 2024. The complete 100-minute film has been released on YouTube under an open-access model, aligning with Menger's legacy of intellectual freedom.
