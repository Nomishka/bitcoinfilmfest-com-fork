## Overview

*Banking on Bitcoin* documents the early emergence of Bitcoin as a revolutionary financial technology, following the key personalities and entrepreneurs who helped launch this decentralized currency movement. Director Christopher Cannucciari interviews Bitcoin pioneers, early investors, and developers navigating the challenges and opportunities of a technology that threatens traditional banking infrastructure.

## Key Themes

- **Financial Innovation**: Bitcoin as peer-to-peer electronic cash, bypassing traditional intermediaries
- **Regulatory Uncertainty**: How governments and financial institutions respond to decentralized currency
- **Early Adoption**: Stories of entrepreneurs and investors placing bets on Bitcoin's future
- **Technological Promise vs. Reality**: Security, scalability, and practical adoption barriers

## Featured Contributors

- **Andreas M. Antonopoulos** — Bitcoin advocate and author
- **Gavin Andresen** — Bitcoin Lead Maintainer
- **Charlie Shrem** — BitInstant founder
- **Roger Ver** — Bitcoin evangelist and investor
- Historical archive featuring Satoshi Nakamoto's writings
