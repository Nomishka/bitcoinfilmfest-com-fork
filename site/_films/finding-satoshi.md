## Overview

*Finding Satoshi* is a groundbreaking investigative documentary that undertakes a four-year evidence-based investigation into Bitcoin's greatest unsolved mystery: Who is Satoshi Nakamoto? 

Featuring investigative journalist William D. Cohan and private investigator Tyler Maroney, the documentary presents their conclusions through rare interviews with key crypto architects and early Bitcoin builders, blending forensic investigation with an accessible exploration of Bitcoin's philosophy and evolution from radical idea to global financial force.

## Key Themes

- **Bitcoin's Origins**: Tracing the creation and ideological roots of Bitcoin
- **Satoshi's Identity**: Evidence-based investigation into cryptocurrency's mysterious creator
- **Radical Ideals**: Examining the philosophical motivations behind Bitcoin's design
- **Crypto Evolution**: How Bitcoin evolved from obscure protocol to global financial phenomenon
- **Cypherpunk Movement**: Historical context of cryptographic freedom advocates
