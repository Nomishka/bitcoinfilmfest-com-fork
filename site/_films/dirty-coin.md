synopsis: >
  A feature documentary that goes inside Bitcoin mining's server rooms, power
  grids and surrounding communities, from rural Texas to the mountains of
  Malawi and across four continents. Interviews with energy experts, miners and
  local residents examine both the controversy around mining's energy use and
  the case for data centres as drivers of energy infrastructure. The feature
  expands Alana Mediavilla's short film STRANDED.
