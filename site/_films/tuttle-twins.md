## Overview

*Tuttle Twins* is an animated educational series that combines entertainment with economic literacy, introducing young viewers to concepts like Bitcoin, entrepreneurship, free markets, and personal freedom through engaging storylines and adventurous characters.

## Series Themes

- **Economic Literacy**: Teaching foundational economics to young audiences
- **Bitcoin & Blockchain**: Introduction to cryptocurrency and decentralized technology
- **Entrepreneurship**: Problem-solving and creating value through business
- **Individual Liberty**: Concepts of freedom, personal responsibility, and self-determination
- **Critical Thinking**: Questioning authority and developing independent thought
- **Real-World Application**: Applying concepts to practical scenarios

## Target Audience

- Children and young teens (ages 7–16)
- Homeschoolers and alternative education
- Families interested in libertarian and Austrian economics
- Bitcoin enthusiasts seeking age-appropriate introductions
