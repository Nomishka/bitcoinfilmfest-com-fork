## Overview

*The Rise and Rise of Bitcoin* chronicles the explosive growth of Bitcoin from 2011 onwards, capturing the energy and chaos of the cryptocurrency movement. Director Nicholas Mross follows Bitcoin enthusiasts, miners, and entrepreneurs as the asset surges in value, creating new millionaires and attracting global attention.

## Key Themes

- **Price Volatility & Speculation**: Bitcoin's dramatic market swings and their impact on investors
- **Mining Culture**: Industrial and basement miners competing for Bitcoin rewards
- **Early Adoption Stories**: First-generation enthusiasts who believed in Bitcoin's potential
- **Mt. Gox Collapse**: The exchange failure that shook confidence in cryptocurrency infrastructure
- **Cultural Phenomenon**: How Bitcoin transcended technical circles to become a cultural movement
