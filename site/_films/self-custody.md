## Overview

**Self Custody** is a 31-minute Bitcoin action-thriller directed by Garrett Patten and Fernando Ferro, released March 3, 2026 across AVOD platforms (Tubi, Plex) and March 10, 2026 on Amazon Prime Video. Described as a "micro-feature" by Patten, the film blends high production values with a timely exploration of Bitcoin's self-custody paradox: decentralization and individual sovereignty carry the burden of sole responsibility.

## Plot & Themes

The film follows Scott (Garrett Patten), a struggling father and business owner facing financial troubles. When he discovers an old job had granted him a Bitcoin bonus in 2014—now worth millions—he sees a path to redemption. But there's a catch: the Bitcoin is locked in a wallet, and he cannot remember the password.

Desperate to access his fortune, Scott turns to his former friend and boss (Adrian Grenier), who connects him with a clandestine operation specializing in breaking into crypto wallets. This decision sets off a cascade of disastrous consequences involving hackers, dark web threats, and the resurfacing of buried secrets.

The film's central tension articulates the double edge of Bitcoin ownership: the technology that liberates users from centralized control simultaneously abandons them to the consequences of their own security failures. Inspired by a real case—a friend of Patten's who lost millions in Bitcoin because he couldn't access the wallet—*Self Custody* examines a phenomenon affecting millions globally: cryptocurrency lost to forgotten passwords, misplaced seed phrases, or simple forgetfulness.

## Reception & Visibility

The film premiered at **SXSW** in March 2026, where Patten participated in a THR Presents Q&A alongside Grenier and Cejudo. Critics and audiences praised its smart tension, high production values, and prescient exploration of Bitcoin's central paradox. On **Letterboxd**, reviewers noted the film's effective 31-minute pacing and Trezor hardware wallet integration as part of its broader self-custody narrative.

**IMDB Rating:** 6.8/10

**Audience Response:** At SXSW, when polled on whether to expand *Self Custody* into a feature film or TV series, audience members voted almost unanimously for a television adaptation. Feature and series adaptations are currently in discussion with Hollywood studios.

## Bitcoin Angle

*Self Custody* is one of the few narrative films that seriously examines Bitcoin's ownership model and its human consequences. Rather than fetishizing Bitcoin as a technological marvel or a get-rich-quick scheme, the film presents both sides: the genuine freedom of peer-to-peer, government-resistant currency and the terrifying vulnerability of irreversible transactions and sole responsibility for security.

The film's tagline—*redemption, technology, and the price of lost access*—captures the emotional core: Bitcoin offers escape from institutional control only for those capable of managing absolute self-custody. For millions of users, that capability is elusive.

Patten noted in interviews: "There are thousands of stories like this around Bitcoin."

## Future

A feature-length adaptation of *Self Custody* is reportedly in development, with interest from multiple Hollywood studios in expanding the narrative into either a feature film or limited series. The 31-minute short has proven compelling enough to warrant deeper exploration.
