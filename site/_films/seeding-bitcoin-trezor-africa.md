synopsis: >
  Seeding Bitcoin profiles educators, merchants, and community members across
  Sub-Saharan Africa using Bitcoin not as speculative asset but as functional
  monetary infrastructure. Featuring Bitcoin education centers in South Africa
  where students complete diploma courses and receive bitcoin stipends; merchants
  who adopted cryptocurrency after learning stablecoin settlement; a woman who
  traveled 14 hours to attend a grassroots Bitcoin conference; and a former
  drug addict whose life shifted through engagement with local circular economies.
  The through-line: financial exclusion. Refugees, orphans, and people without
  formal addresses or government ID cannot access banks. Bitcoin "doesn't
  recognize if you're poor or rich, what color your skin is, or whether you have
  ID." Chainalysis recorded $205 billion in on-chain value across Sub-Saharan
  Africa (mid-2025), up 52% YoY—third-fastest regional growth globally. Remittance
  fees (9% via traditional channels, cents on Lightning) and currency instability
  drive adoption. Trezor Academy has run 300+ meetups, graduated 2,000+ students
  across 30+ countries, training local educators to build circular economies
  ground-up.
bitcoin_angle: >
  The documentary reframes Bitcoin from speculation to practical infrastructure,
  centering on communities excluded from traditional finance where Bitcoin
  serves as medium of exchange, store of value, and savings vehicle outside
  government-controlled currency. Trezor Academy's model—training local educators
  in native languages to build decentralized networks—demonstrates how Bitcoin
  adoption scales through grassroots, community-led education rather than
  top-down implementation. The film directly addresses financial exclusion:
  refugees, orphans, and undocumented populations gain access to permissionless
  payment and savings infrastructure through Bitcoin and Lightning Network.
  With Sub-Saharan Africa showing highest proportion of sub-$10k transactions
  globally, the data validates ground-level stories of retail adoption driven
  by real economic necessity, not speculation.
