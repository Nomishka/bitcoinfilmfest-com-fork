synopsis: >
  Many have asked the question, "What is Bitcoin?" Few have ever dared to ask,
  "Who is Bitcoin?" This short film introduces Bitty — a seven-foot-tall
  anthropomorphic Bitcoin puppet with articulated brows and a moving mouth —
  built by Spiral to explain Bitcoin's purpose through character and story
  rather than lectures. Bitty was deliberately designed grumpy because "kids
  latch onto grouches, and there is a long puppet tradition of it." The film
  uses real puppetry (a person inside the suit) on purpose, to be surprised by
  what happened on camera. It passed half a million views and spawned a
  creative universe of 100+ license-free Bitcoin character assets (SVG/PNG) at
  herecomesbitcoin.org, plus a plushie, stickers, and a memecoin ($BITTY) that
  donates 100% of trade taxes to bitcoin.org.
bitcoin_angle: >
  The film reframes Bitcoin education from "what it costs" to "what it's for" —
  using a tangible, grumpy puppet character to make Bitcoin approachable for
  newcomers. The Here Comes Bitcoin project provides 100+ free creative assets
  (SVG/PNG, license-free) so anyone can use the Bitcoin character in their own
  projects, memes, education, or merchandise. This is Bitcoin culture as open
  source IP: the puppet, the short film, the asset library, and the community
  all serve onboarding rather than speculation.
