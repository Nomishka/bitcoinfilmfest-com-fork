## About

**The Fall of 2008** is the second installment in Jimmy Morrison's award-winning trilogy examining financial crises that exposed systemic failures in US monetary policy. Following the success of *The Housing Bubble* (2019), which won an Audience Award at Anthem Film Festival, this sequel deepens the investigation into the 2008 financial crisis and its aftermath.

## Narrative & Themes

Co-written by libertarian economist Tom Woods, the film documents how Federal Reserve actions and government bailouts designed to prevent immediate collapse ultimately prolonged the recovery period, creating the slowest economic rebound since the Great Depression. Rather than allowing market corrections, the film argues, intervention prevented genuine healing.

The documentary features interviews and commentary from leading libertarian and Austrian school economists who successfully predicted the housing crash and financial crisis years before they occurred—figures including Ron Paul, Jim Rogers, Peter Schiff, Marc Faber, Doug Casey, Jim Grant, and David Stockman. Their analyses contrast sharply with mainstream economic consensus and government policy responses.

## Bitcoin Connection

As the second film in the trilogy, *The Fall of 2008* contextualizes the financial events that directly preceded Bitcoin's creation. By examining the policy failures and mismanagement exposed during the crisis, the film illuminates why decentralized, monetary alternatives became necessary—connecting to Bitcoin's founding principle of removing trusted intermediaries from money systems.

## Creator

**Jimmy Morrison** is an award-winning independent documentary filmmaker and podcaster. He founded Let Us Disagree Films in 2007 and hosts the podcast *Let Us Disagree with Jimmy Morrison* (available on Spotify, iHeart, Apple Podcasts). He has also been a successful advocate for medical marijuana policy reform in Iowa.
