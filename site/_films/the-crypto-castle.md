## Overview

*The Crypto Castle* is a documentary series examining Rory Johnston's ambitious project to establish a Bitcoin-focused hub and sanctuary in Germany. The series follows the vision, challenges, and real-world obstacles of building a community centered around Bitcoin, cryptocurrency, and digital freedom principles.

## Series Arc

- **Vision & Idealism**: The dream of a Bitcoin sanctuary and decentralized community
- **Practical Implementation**: Navigating regulatory frameworks and local government
- **Community Building**: Attracting like-minded technologists and entrepreneurs
- **Cultural Clash**: Traditional governance vs. decentralized decision-making
- **Lessons Learned**: The gap between cryptographic freedom and social reality

## Key Themes

- **Bitcoin Communities**: How Bitcoin enthusiasts organize and collaborate
- **Digital Sovereignty**: Practical challenges of implementing cryptocurrency-based governance
- **Regulatory Environment**: How governments respond to cryptocurrency hubs
- **Idealism vs. Reality**: The tension between technological vision and practical constraints
- **Global Bitcoin Culture**: European perspective on cryptocurrency adoption
