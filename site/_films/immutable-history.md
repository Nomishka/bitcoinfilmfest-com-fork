## Overview

*Immutable History* explores the philosophical and practical implications of Bitcoin's immutable blockchain as a tool for historical preservation and truth-telling. The documentary argues that Bitcoin's permanent, distributed ledger offers a solution to state-controlled narratives and information manipulation.

## Key Themes

- **Censorship Resistance**: How Bitcoin's technology prevents erasure of historical records
- **Immutability & Truth**: Creating verifiable, unchangeable historical records on blockchain
- **State Narratives vs. Decentralized Truth**: The role of Bitcoin in countering revisionism
- **Cypherpunk Philosophy**: History of cryptographic tools designed to protect privacy and freedom
- **Truth as Political Act**: How permanent records challenge authority and power structures
