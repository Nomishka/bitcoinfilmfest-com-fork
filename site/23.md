    <div class="bff23-inner">
      <p class="bff23-section-label">01 · the beginning</p>
      <h2>Before the archive had an archive.</h2>
      <div class="bff23-story-grid">
        <div>
          <p class="bff23-lead-copy">BFF’23 ran from <strong>24–26 March 2023</strong> at Kinoteka, inside Warsaw’s Palace of Culture and Science. Contemporaneous public promotion called it the world’s first Bitcoin FilmFest.</p>
          <p>The available record is strongest on the films, conversations, workshops and closing awards. It is deliberately quiet on details the sources do not settle: there is no reconstructed timetable here, and the internal attendance estimate is not treated as a public total.</p>
        </div>

  <section class="bff23-band bff23-band--paper" id="programme">
    <div class="bff23-inner">
      <p class="bff23-section-label">02 · programme shape</p>
      <h2>Open rooms for films and ideas.</h2>
      <p class="bff23-band-lede">The public evidence supports a community-led format: screenings paired with filmmaker or subject conversations, an Open Bitcoin Workshop strand, and a closing winners ceremony.</p>
      <div class="bff23-programme-grid">
        <article><span class="bff23-number">01</span><h3>Film screenings</h3><p>Documentaries and feature-length stories about Bitcoin, money, freedom and the people building around them formed the core of the Warsaw programme.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff23-number">02</span><h3>Open Bitcoin Workshops</h3><p>Open Bitcoin Workshops were scheduled and documented during BFF’23. The surviving record does not establish a single definitive session list.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff23-number">03</span><h3>Conversations after films</h3><p><em>Human B</em> was followed by a filmmaker panel; <em>The Great Reset and the Rise of Bitcoin</em> by a mining-film panel; and the final panel discussed <em>The Satoshi Mystery</em>.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff23-number">04</span><h3>Weekend Kapitalizmu</h3><p><em>A Sly Roundabout Way</em> was promoted as a BFF’23 screening during the Weekend Kapitalizmu period. It is listed as programme context, not assigned an invented time.</p></article>
      </div>
