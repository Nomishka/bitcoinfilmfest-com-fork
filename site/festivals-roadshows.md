    <h1>Minis &amp; roadshows</h1>
    <p>Bitcoin FilmFest can travel as a compact cinema programme: films, short works, creators and conversation shaped around a local community or a larger Bitcoin event.</p>
  </header>

  <section class="reel-section">
    <p class="section-label">The format</p>
    <h2>A cinema that can move</h2>
    <p>The archive describes a flexible format rather than a second full-scale festival: one or two programme blocks, a longer central screening, shorts or trailers, and a conversation with filmmakers or local guests. Details below are intentionally broad until final programmes and public event records are checked.</p>
  </section>
