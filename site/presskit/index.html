04 · Usage

How to Use These

Filmmakers, press & partners: free to use on your festival page, posters, trailers, social and print. Please do not modify the colours or proportions of the laurels and logos. The black version of the laurels is for light backgrounds; the white version is for dark backgrounds.

Need a different format, size or colour? Drop us a note at hello@bitcoinfilmfest.com and we'll send it.
