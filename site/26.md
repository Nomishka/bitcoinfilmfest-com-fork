                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">BITCHAMLER — FREEDOM ON THE RIVER</div>
                  <div class="desc">On the Danube, a Serbian crew converts river scrap metal into Bitcoin, building a small circular economy where ecology and monetary sovereignty meet. A documentary short on waste, recycling and orange-pilled river life.</div>
                  <div class="meta">Belgrade Bitcoin community · 25 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">BITCOIN CASTLE</div>
                  <div class="desc">In a remote French castle, Astrid and Henning's marriage frays as Henning — a Bitcoin prepper hoarding 42 BTC and canned food — waits for the crash. A conspiracy threatens both stash and relationship. German black-comedy thriller (Schloss Bitcoin).</div>
                  <div class="meta">Bruno Schiebel · 28 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">BITCOIN MOTORCYCLE DIARIES</div>
                  <div class="desc">A one-hour 'videozine' from Bitcoin Video Magazine — stories, characters and conversations from the global Bitcoin road. A short on-stage chapter screens at the festival; the full edition follows on VOD.</div>
                  <div class="meta">Bitcoin Video Magazine · ~60 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">COINED IN WATERCOLOR</div>
                  <div class="desc">An eleven-minute Bitcoin-community piece — raw, emotionally human, more about spirit and culture than coins. Features watercolour artist Timothy Murphy and original music from Murphy Robertson, capturing maturing Bitcoin art beyond memes and ticker-watching.</div>
                  <div class="meta">Gavin Robertson · 12 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">CRYING BABY, DANCING BEE</div>
                  <div class="desc">Buzzbee, the African Bitcoin Queen, returns from Jamaica to South Africa, reconnecting with nature on Table Mountain before travelling through a Tree of Life into the Bitcoin Cathedral. A playful animated journey through community, mining, food, ecology and the waggle dance.</div>
                  <div class="meta">Short · 11 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">CRYPTO CASTLE</div>
                  <div class="desc">San Francisco, 2015, Bitcoin at $250: a young woman accidentally moves into a hacker house with four crypto-obsessed roommates. A satirical autobiographical period-piece comedy web series capturing the culture, idealism and absurdity of early Bitcoin adopters.</div>
                  <div class="meta">Viv Ford · 9 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">FINDING HOME — EP. 4</div>
                  <div class="desc">Episode four of a documentary series on Bitcoiners finding a sovereign home through food, freedom and family. This instalment profiles Roger9000 — multi-instrumentalist and BFF resident artist — tracing his music, his life and his journey down the rabbit hole.</div>
                  <div class="meta">Avi Burra · 32 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">FINDING SATOSHI</div>
                  <div class="desc">A four-year evidence-led investigation into the identity of Satoshi Nakamoto. Investigative journalist William D. Cohan (NYT bestselling author) and private investigator Tyler Maroney (QRI) trace Bitcoin's origins through rare interviews with Michael Saylor, Joseph Lubin, Fred Ehrsam, Bill Gates, Phil Zimmermann, Bram Cohen, Jameson Lopp, Kara Swisher, Bjarne Stroustrup and many more — and the film confidently identifies the person behind Bitcoin. <strong>At BFF’26 we screened the trailer and the film featured in the program. More at <a href="https://www.findingsatoshi.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">findingsatoshi.com</a>.</strong></div>
                  <div class="meta">Matthew Miele &amp; Tucker Tooley · 101 min</div>
                </article>
                <article class="program-item">
                  <div class="title">FROM EL SALVADOR TO GLOBAL MISSION</div>
                  <div class="desc">From the El Zonte fishing village that sparked El Salvador's legal-tender adoption to the global wave of grassroots Bitcoin communities it inspired — a documentary tracing how one small Pacific-coast experiment grew into an international movement.</div>
                  <div class="meta">Documentary · 30 min</div>
                </article>

              <p>Bitcoin FilmFest (BFF) is the largest film festival dedicated to Bitcoin culture. Founded in Warsaw in November 2022, it's a grassroots, community-driven gathering that brings filmmakers, artists, technologists and curious newcomers together around stories of money, freedom and the future. BFF’26 is the fourth edition.</p>
              <p>BFF is not a conference with films bolted on — it's a cultural event built around cinema. The program spans feature documentaries, dramas, comedies, shorts and AI-assisted films, alongside workshops, panels, a Community Stage shared with MoneroKon, an AI short-film contest with a 2.5M-sat prize pool, art exhibitions, and daily social events. The festival's own awards, the Golden Rabbits, are presented at a closing gala.</p>
