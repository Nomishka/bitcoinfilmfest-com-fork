  <section class="bff27-band bff27-band--dark" id="story">
    <div class="bff27-inner">
      <p class="bff27-section-label">01 · what is BFF?</p>
      <h2>A festival for the stories outside the frame.</h2>
      <div class="bff27-story-grid">
        <div>
          <p class="bff27-lead-copy">Bitcoin FilmFest is a Warsaw-born gathering for films, filmmakers, artists, builders and curious people who see Bitcoin as a cultural movement, not just a piece of technology.</p>
          <p>It started in November 2022. Since then it has grown into the grassroots heart of Bitcoin cinema: one cinema, four days, screenings and conversations by day, long nights with people you did not know before the doors opened.</p>
          <p>You do not need to be a Bitcoiner to come. You only need to be curious.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="bff27-fact-strip">
          <span><strong>24–27 June</strong>2027 · Thursday to Sunday</span>
          <span><strong>Kinoteka</strong>Palace of Culture, Warsaw</span>
          <span><strong>5th edition</strong>since 2022</span>
        </div>
      </div>

      <h3 class="bff27-subhead">The last edition, BFF’26, in numbers</h3>
      <div class="bff27-stats">

        <p><strong>15+</strong><span>countries</span></p>
        <p><strong>500+</strong><span>Bitcoin &amp; Lightning payments</span></p>
      </div>
      <p>Titles at different stages of development, including concepts.</p>

  <section class="bff27-band bff27-band--paper" id="expect">
    <div class="bff27-inner">
      <p class="bff27-section-label">what to expect</p>
      <h2>Come for the films. Stay for the people.</h2>
      <p class="bff27-band-lede">Based on how BFF’26 ran. The BFF’27 programme is not announced yet, so this is the shape of the festival, not the line-up.</p>
      <ul class="bff27-expect">
        <li><strong>Films</strong><span>Features, documentaries, shorts and experiments from filmmakers around the world, with directors in the room.</span></li>
        <li><strong>Panels &amp; talks</strong><span>Conversations about money, culture, media and what Bitcoin changes for storytellers.</span></li>
        <li><strong>Workshops</strong><span>Hands-on sessions: AI filmmaking, Lightning builds and more, for beginners and pros.</span></li>
        <li><strong>Music &amp; parties</strong><span>Live sets and DJs, an opening party, an after-party every night.</span></li>
        <li><strong>Golden Rabbits</strong><span>The festival awards, voted at the festival and handed out at the closing gala.</span></li>
        <li><strong>Bitcoin, for real</strong><span>Pay for things with Bitcoin and Lightning, meet the community, and try it yourself.</span></li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>
