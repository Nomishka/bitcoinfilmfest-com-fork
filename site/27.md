      <p class="bff27-section-label">01 · what is BFF?</p>
      <h2>A festival for the stories outside the frame.</h2>
      <div class="bff27-story-grid">
        <div>
          <p class="bff27-lead-copy">Bitcoin FilmFest is a Warsaw-born gathering for films, filmmakers, artists, builders and curious people who see Bitcoin as a cultural movement, not just a piece of technology.</p>
          <p>There are screenings, conversations, workshops, music, art, side events and long nights with people you did not know before the doors opened. The exact shape of BFF’27 is still being made.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="bff27-fact-strip">
