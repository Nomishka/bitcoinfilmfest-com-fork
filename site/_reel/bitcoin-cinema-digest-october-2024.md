# Since many of you are probably preparing their meetups to celebrate Bitcoin WhitePaper Day, we hope that this issue sparks some cinematic inspiration along the way. Let’s go!

*October 28, 2024*

## Catch Up: A Peek From a Few Weeks Ago

**#01:** A week ago, after a long break, we finally had our first live session. Two filmmakers [Alana Mediavilla](https://x.com/AlanaMediavilla) and [Max DeMarco](https://x.com/itsmaxdemarco), as well as Zack Mahoney, the founder of the [IndeeHub Studio](https://x.com/indeehub). Topic? ‘Independent Cinema: The Journey from Idea to Screen’. Over an hour of lively chit-chat and plenty of interesting takeaways. Check it out by watching its recording [on ZapStream](https://zap.stream/naddr1qqjxzvehxsckzdmx94skyv3e956rzetp95ukvdpj95mrscnpxgcnvdfcxgckzqgewaehxw309aex2mrp0yh8xmn0wf6zuum0vd5kzmp0qgsv73dxhgfk8tt76gf6q788zrfyz9dwwgwfk3aar6l5gk82a76v9fgrqsqqqan8h8fvr6), or [download the audio here](https://drive.proton.me/urls/MQDQYHVXT4).

**#02:** In the first half of October, HBO released its new film, [MONEY ELECTRIC: THE BITCOIN MYSTERY](https://www.hbo.com/movies/money-electric-the-bitcoin-mystery). Regardless of mixed opinions, even controversies, it’s definitely an influential and technically a very well made film.

**#03:** October brought several Binema screenings to Europe and the U.S. Two of them, as an example, are award-winning documentaries: [Paul Kemp](https://x.com/PaulKemp800)’s **SEARCHING FOR SATOSH**I in NYC, Williamsburg Cinema, and Alana’s **DIRTY COIN** in Ketelhuis, Amsterdam. If you’re interested in the first title and don’t mind watching it at home on a smaller screen, head over to [IndeeHub](https://indeehub.studio/). As for DIRTY COIN, there are many [more sessions planned](https://gathr.com/films/dirty-coin-the-bitcoin-mining-documentary/screenings) in different locations worldwide.

Great, isn’t it? [More details here](https://geyser.fund/grants/12).

## Bitcoin Cinema: Reels to Keep an Eye On

📽️ **PLANDEMIC: The Musical**

A short but engaging movie, actually part of an entire series that has been released on October 10th (free to watch + download). “A powerful reminder that we are the creators of our own future”. [Get it here](https://plandemicseries.com/)!

📽️ **HUMMINGBIRD. THE BITCOIN JUNGLE STORY**

A new documentary film that’s on the horizon. Blending the beautiful scenery of Costa Rica, local prophecies, and the value of Bitcoin. Check out [its trailer in this post](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1839629905157357603)!

📽️ **BLOCK 170**

A short production from Amberverse. A tribute and surreal representation of Bitcoin and the first financial transaction between Satoshi and Hal Finney. Though it’s already 3 years old, it’s still [worth a watch](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1839228919997682096).

📽️ **CAPTIVATING SHORTS**

More and more videos from [Ioni Appelberg](https://x.com/IoniAppelberg), not only packed with knowledge but also deep philosophical insights, are being added regularly to his new series. Watch them directly on [his YT channel](https://www.youtube.com/@IoniAppelberg/videos).

There are many rabbit holes worth exploring… film is definitely one of them 🕳️🐇
