# Bitcoin FilmFest Joins Bitcoin News for an Exclusive Chat

The Bitcoin FilmFest team sat down with Bitcoin News for a candid conversation about the festival's mission, values, and the role of Bitcoin in reshaping independent cinema.

The discussion also covered Bitcoin's core philosophy, the challenges and successes of running a Bitcoin-only film festival, and what excited the team most about BFF25, then scheduled for that May in Warsaw.

**Read the full interview at [BitcoinNews.com](https://bitcoinnews.com/interviews/bitcoin-news-interview-bitcoin-filmfest/).**

*About Bitcoin News: Bitcoin News Inc is a US-based company specializing in media and marketing for Bitcoin. With a strong history of managing a news platform and producing articles about Bitcoin fundamental analysis, the team has established itself as a key player in advancing Bitcoin education.*

[Bitcoin FilmFest](https://bitcoinfilmfest.com/about/) is a grassroots project dedicated to independent cinema and culture, aiming to promote creative expression, original perspectives, and a sovereign future. Held annually in Warsaw, the main edition has grown into one of the notable events on the Bitcoin cinema calendar since it began in 2023.
