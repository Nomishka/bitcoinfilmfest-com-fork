## BFF Buzz: Hot News + Key Updates

**#01:** Geyser Fund launched a new program – exclusive perks and direct support for independent creators (including many filmmakers). Your contribution helps drive their mission forward. [Learn more and consider becoming one of the Guardians](https://cze0h.r.a.d.sendibm1.com/mk/cl/f/sh/1f8JIKXwHGYox99Kaqk6eZjPAV/H0M4TIRuZuaD)!

**#02:** You might have already heard that May 22–25 is locked in for the next edition of Bitcoin FilmFest. The initial mention, placing us among some decent conference ‘neighbors’, has already been [published by BitBo Tools](https://cze0h.r.a.d.sendibm1.com/mk/cl/f/sh/1f8JIKXwHGZDuLoXY1FxuwloGd/yzJy-A07u0mg). Stay tuned **#BFF25**. More details coming soon.

**#03:** The first virtual screening of DIRTY COIN (one of the winning titles at BFF24), followed by a live Q&A with director Alana Mediavilla, is set for January 10, 2025. [Save your spot here](https://cze0h.r.a.d.sendibm1.com/mk/cl/f/sh/1f8JIKXwHGZcrYTkVBlpBJoDMl/wuHzxo6YrM_y)!

[Alana Mediavilla - BFF24](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/nicehashx-slovenja-with-bitcoin-bitcoin-filmfest/)

**Site note + HUGE congrats:** **Julio Cesar Torres, editor of DC, received [an Emmy award this year](https://cze0h.r.a.d.sendibm1.com/mk/cl/f/sh/1f8JIKXwHGa1ol8xSMHgRgqcSt/H8KpaaaDtZCV), ‘Best Director’ for one of the other films he worked on. Another reason to join the session in January – an epic team equals a very good film!**

**#04:** Wrapping up the year with exciting news: In collaboration with [Juan Cienfuegos](https://x.com/TheJuanSC) (BitCorner Podcast), once a month, we’ll be releasing a unique episode featuring different filmmakers to discuss inspiration behind their work, untold stories, fun facts, and more…

**The very first recording, with [Bitcoin Shooter](https://x.com/BitcoinShooter), dedicated to his latest film, NO MORE INFLATION, is already live 🎙️ Check it out on YT, Rumble, X, or Fountain App.**
