# Ready for the wrap-up of last month? This January we have for you: 2 announcements, 4 updates, 10 titles to explore.

*January 28, 2025*

📣 **ANNOUNCEMENT** 🔔

[The Catapult Development Grant](https://catapultfilmfund.org/how-to-apply/development/) ($5k – $25k for ‘independent filmmakers who have a strong story to tell + are ready to unlock critical production funding’. Apply: 15 Jan- 12 Feb.

— **BFF BUZZ: Orange Lens on Culture —**

**#01:** As of Jan 21, Ross Ulbricht became a free man! [GetBased](https://x.com/getbasedtv) released [a special video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNzyaxizrNo) dedicated to this topic.

**#02:** GOD BLESS BITCOIN got a new set of subtitles to make their film accessible to an even wider audience (13 languages, [FREE to watch](https://www.youtube.com/@GodBlessBitcoin)).

**#03:** [Yannis Stergioulis](https://x.com/YannisSter), a professional trailer editor and colourist, started [a daily newsletter](https://yannis-stergioulis.kit.com/posts) sharing 1-minute insights based on his 20+ years of experience.

**#04:** Our team kicked off an [amateur series of shorts](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/bitcoin-film-fest-short-videos-why-warsaw-why-bff-part-our-one-rebel-base/) to bring you closer to Warsaw + BFF. Each episode to be followed by a live Q&A. Check out the first video by [Kasia](https://x.com/itsKasiaS).

— **Bitcoin Cinema: Reels to Keep an Eye On** —

🎞️ **WHAT’S THE PROBLEM**: A 39-minute presentation by [Joe Bryan](https://x.com/satmojoe) that has taken the entire space by storm. [Watch it here](https://x.com/SatsVsFiat) if you haven’t had a chance yet. Share with those who still doubt ‘why Bitcoin’.

🎞️ **ANIMATED SHORTS**: Ambitious AI-videos by BurnTheFed (aka [Jefferson](https://x.com/BurnTheFed)) inspired by history and the message of freedom. Visit [his channel on Rumble](https://rumble.com/user/BurnTheFed/videos).

🎞️ **TUTTLE TWINS**: Season 3 Episode 8, “Meatball Smears & War Profiteers” will have its livestream premiere on Feb 05. [Don’t miss out](https://www.angel.com/livestreams/173da038-e306-48c6-ba5f-e02128da47e5)!

🎞️ **2073**: A new film by award-winning director Asif Kapadia. ‘This is not fiction. This is not a documentary. This is a warning’. Available on Apple TV, Amazon TV + other platforms. [Details here.](https://neonrated.com/films/2073)

🎞️ **WHO REALLY OWNS THE FEDERAL RESERVE?**: A short documentary by [GetBased](https://x.com/getbasedtv). ‘What if the greatest threat to our freedom was created in secret by a handful of bankers on a remote island?’. Watch it [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zw9rg8PU0W8).

🎥 **SATOSHI PLANET** (work in progress): Possibly the craziest Bitcoin cartoon series ever made. Visit its active [campaign on Geyser Fund](https://geyser.fund/project/satoshiplanet?hero=geyser). Follow, share it with others and consider sending some sats.
