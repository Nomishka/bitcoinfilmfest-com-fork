### Hello Orange Rabbits! We've heard you like movies and love Bitcoin!

We hope this newsletter finds you well! We're reaching out with gratitude and exciting news from Bitcoin FilmFest — the epicenter of the Bitcoin cinema world.

### 💛 Our Heartfelt Thank You… FREE TICKETS

Firstly, we want to express our heartfelt appreciation for your interest in the Bitcoin FilmFest. Our mission is to curate and share thought-provoking, Bitcoin-inspired films. We believe that cinema has the power to capture the zeitgeist of the Bitcoin movement and highlight its potential in ways no other medium can.

As promised, once a month we're randomly choosing one of you to get a free ticket to the Bitcoin FilmFest 2024 in Warsaw! This month, the Maxi Ticket goes to… a lucky subscriber. Congratulations and see you in Warsaw!

### ⏰ Bitcoin Cinema News

- New film announcement by Alex Waltz: **Satoshis Don't Exist** — the movie will delve into the unconventional and less-known design decisions behind Bitcoin and their impact on the construction of projects built on it.
- Coming soon: **Dirty Coin: The Controversy Behind Bitcoin Mining** — the film about Bitcoin mining is coming out in late 2023/early 2024.
- Bitcoin Maxis Club is raising funds on Geyser for their new cartoon! Support one of the Bitcoin FilmFest partners and see you in Warsaw.
- **Lekker Feeling: A Bitcoin Ekasi Story** documentary released on YouTube — the movie takes you to Mossel Bay, South Africa, where a group called Bitcoin Ekasi has partnered with a local surfing group to create new opportunities with Bitcoin.
- **Searching For Satoshi** to be released October 29 on CBC — a new feature documentary by Paul Kemp investigates the disappearance of Satoshi Nakamoto.
- Bitcoin in Mexico is a documentary series aiming to educate different audiences by showing how Bitcoin changes and improves their lives. Chris, the director, sent us behind-the-scenes footage from Merida, where he is shooting the next episode.
- New NOSTR documentary released by Max de Marco — find out why social media is broken and enjoy the documentary on YouTube.
- **The Bitcoin Executor** by Christopher Arcella finished shooting! The first fiction feature film about Bitcoin should be released before the Halving.

### 🥳 Bitcoin FilmFest 2024… WHEN?

We're elated to announce the dates for the next Bitcoin FilmFest! Join us in Warsaw from April 19th to 21st, 2024, for a celebration of Bitcoin and film at BFF'24. We also expect block #840,000 to be mined around that time… so get ready for the **European Halving Party**!

But we don't have to wait that long to meet you — the BFF team and films will be present at Adopting Bitcoin in Salvador, Plan B Lugano, and a few more events. Stay tuned!

### 📸 Snapshots from BTC Prague

To help us on this quest, we've been broadening our horizons and networking with like-minded people at BTC Prague in June. We represented Bitcoin FilmFest there — catch a glimpse of the excitement in the photo gallery from the conference.

### 🎥 Bitcoin FilmClub #2 starts… TOMORROW!

We're trying out our own podcast/TV show/streamed videocall of Bitcoin filmmakers — episode #2 already! Bitcoin FilmClub is a live video show from behind the screen of Bitcoin Cinema. This time, we met with three filmmakers, each at a different stage of production: Alex Waltz with *Satoshis Don't Exist*, Rob Johnson with *Bitcoiners*, and Pierre Corbin with *The Fight for U.S. Dollar*.

Thank you for being an integral part of this journey as we continue to foster and grow the Bitcoin cinema industry. Stay tuned for more exciting updates from Bitcoin FilmFest!
