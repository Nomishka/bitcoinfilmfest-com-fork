## BFF-Buzz: What's Hot and Happening These Days?

**#01:** The premiere of [GOD BLESS BITCOIN](https://x.com/godblessBTCfilm%20), the new film so many are waiting for, is coming up. It’ll be [available online, free to watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oksraL7wN6Q). Mark your calendars for July 25!

**#02:** Speaking of premieres: If you missed watching the biggest Spanish film about Bitcoin, ([REVOLUCIÓN BITCOIN](https://x.com/revolucionconb)), with its 1st public screening last month, as well as those extra options in Valencia and Madrid, let’s talk.

**For those who want to watch this film outside of Spain or even Europe, leave a comment under** [our post](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1813191854470426832).

**#03:** As for ‘on-demand screenings’, you have a chance to help with the next one of [DIRTY COIN](https://x.com/DirtyCoinDoc). Details? See [this post](https://x.com/DirtyCoinDoc/status/1808004514764505360).

**#04:** To continue with new titles worth your attention, [FORGING A COUNTRY](https://x.com/jessicasolce/status/1812936357897249075), a new film by Jessica Solce, will premiere next month (Aug 15th: premiere in El Salvador + Aug 16th: online release).

A Bitcoin book with a fictional plot that’s turning into a movie? Yes! It’s happening with some help from AI. Check out the [trailer here.](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1813574626900910474)

Another brilliant case of [AI-generated film](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1811070048796119450). Let’s not forget that behind any tech, there’s always a talented person! Kudos to [Arthur Machado](https://x.com/arthurmachado).

One more example of how AI can support independent creators. A captivating [38 min watch](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1812068452212851137) based on a piece written by [Tomer Strolight](https://x.com/TomerStrolight).
