Bitcoin cinema doesn't only live on screen. A lot of the best context — why a film got made, what it took to finish it, what the filmmaker actually believes — comes out in conversation. Here's a roundup of podcast and interview appearances by directors, producers and creators behind Bitcoin films, some embedded below, the rest linked so you can go straight to the source.

## More conversations worth your time {#more-conversations}

A few of these creators sat down more than once, and a few more didn't make the embed list above but are worth the click:

- **Graham Rittener**, *Dare to Dream* — also on [BitCorner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fnbXBxtmTdw).
- **Jessica Solce**, *Death Athletic* — also on [Liberty Under Attack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8bUPAFOUvn4).
- **Brian Estes**, *God Bless Bitcoin* — producer, on [Robert Breedlove's podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8ltJ2hphlUY), alongside **Mike Hill** talking storytelling and cinema in the same conversation.
- **Pedro G.**, *The Anatomy of Bitcoin* — on [Bitcoin Rapid-Fire](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=biU4IneUj-4) and [High Hash Rate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zj53pOZeZ7A).
- **Terence Michael**, on legacy cinema and Bitcoin — [Once BITten](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RAPnPlwBmiU).
- **Joel Ozborn**, *Batman: Knight In Lockdown* — creator and filmmaker, on [Bitcoin Made Simple](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QcadUwVoEkc).
- **Steve L. and Haley Berkoe**, *Here Comes Bitcoin* — on [What Bitcoin Did](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fJiK4d5sy6Y).
- **Rob Johnson and guests**, *Bitcoiners* — on [BTC Sessions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tr7elsk2-r0).
- **Jenna Reid**, *Where Do We Go From Here?* — filmmaker, on [AccessTribe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gVKbbcqGJuc).
- **Alex Waltz**, *Satoshis Don't Exist* — on [High Hash Rate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Pr6NspyO9k).
- **Daniel Harmon**, *Tuttle Twins TV* — on [BitCorner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iwal2EUIYnE) and [BlockHash Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUYkdrqA6_U).
- **Matt Osterman**, *Bitcon* — filmmaker, on [Show Don't Tell: A Podcast On Micro-Budget Filmmaking](https://www.spreaker.com/episode/filmmaker-entrepreneur-matt-osterman-on-his-new-feature-film-bitcon--52083111).

A couple of the source records only had a partial handle for the guest, so a name or two above is listed as given rather than guessed at. If you know one of these people or shows and can confirm a detail, tell us — [get in touch](https://bitcoinfilmfest.com/join/).
