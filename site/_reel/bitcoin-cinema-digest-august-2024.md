*August 23, 2024*

## Catch Up: A Peek From a Few Weeks Ago

**#01:** Last week, Riga was buzzing with Bitcoiners ([#BH2024](https://x.com/search?q=%23BH2024&src=typed_query)) and Nostriches ([#NostRiga](https://x.com/search?q=%23NostRiga&src=typed_query&f=top)). We were right there! CONGRATS to everyone behind those two awesome events. We also had the pleasure of supporting one of the afterparties, [#NostrPeopleConnect](https://x.com/search?q=%23NostrPeopleConnect&src=typed_query&f=top), organized by one of our founders: KUDOS to [ItsTomek](https://x.com/itsTomekK) 💜👏 If you missed all the fun, you can still catch [the broadcast video](https://x.com/getAlby/status/1827039212555645413).

**#02:** While waiting for the full film, it’s worth checking out its trailer and listening to [some of the soundtrack](https://x.com/BitcoinExecutor/status/1824503656948920696) 🎞️ One thing is sure: [BITCOIN EXECUTOR](https://x.com/BitcoinExecutor) has been hard at work this summer.

**— TRAILER —**

**#03:** [Geyser Fund](https://x.com/geyserfund) has introduced a new feature to their platform: An ‘umbrella’ for multiple pages, allowing creators to link their works and making it easier for users to find related stuff in one place. A great example of this in action is [Jessica Solce](https://x.com/jessicasolce) with her projects. Visit [Encode Productions](https://geyser.fund/profile/11263) to see how it works.

## Bitcoin Cinema: Reels to Keep an Eye On

📽️ **UNBANKABLE**

A new inspiring film on the horizon: 7 Years, 7 Countries, 7 African Majority Film Crews, and 1 Question: CAN AFRICA SAVE THE WEST? [Here you can read more about and check out the trailer](https://unbankablemovie.com/).

📽️ **FORGING A COUNTRY**

[A new documentary by Jessica Solce](https://x.com/jessicasolce/status/1821563531726131350) that captures the mood on the streets and behind the scenes in El Salvador leading up to President Bukele’s historic landslide re-election.

📽️ **JONES PLANTATION**

Intriguing, thought-provoking, and somewhat funny. A story that shows how enslaved people can be given a false sense of freedom that they come to believe in. Another proof that Binema isn’t always directly about Bitcoin. [A must-see](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1824733908736651343)!

📽️ **21 VOICES**

Do you already know the series of short films by Swan Studio made in collaboration with talented creators and a few respected faces in the Bitcoin space? Just recently they released final episodes, now covering a total of 21 topics. Head over to [their YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoB1eZWSVHVYnXEMptb3ZHUpgWWp-KfZI) to watch them all.

📽️ **INSPIRING SHORTS**

Still on the subject of short videos that offer wise thoughts, education, and even motivation: Check out [the new series from Ioni Appelberg](https://www.youtube.com/@IoniAppelberg/videos). In just 4 weeks, he has released 4 powerful episodes, with still more likely on the way.

🎬 **THE BITCOIN SOCIAL LAYER DOCUMENATRY**

One of the cool projects, still in progress, that needs your support: A film exploring how Bitcoin is much more than a technology or a financial asset, but an unstoppable idea that will change the world. Visit [their Geyser campaign](https://geyser.fund/project/thebitcoinsociallayerdocumentary), and please consider sending some sats. No amount is small.
