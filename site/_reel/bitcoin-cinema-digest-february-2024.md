### Bitcoin Cinema: The Reel to Keep an Eye On!

- **THE ADVENTURES OF JONATHAN GULLIBLE** — from a book on a free-market odyssey to on-screen animations, this new cartoon series follows an adventurous 18-year-old boy who gets stranded on a foreign island after a storm and encounters plenty of surprises.
- **LEBANON, LIFE AFTER THE STATE** — the fifth part of the "Follow the Money" series by Peter McCormack is now ready to watch. How is Lebanon rebuilding itself in the aftermath of a collapsed government?
- **NO MORE INFLATION**, the new film from Bitcoin Shooter — after a period of silence, it's finally time to shed some light on his latest production. If you're attending Bitcoin Atlantis this March, you'll have the opportunity to experience it first-hand.
- **BITCOINERS** recently premiered in the USA — if Austin, Texas was too far for you, no worries! You can still catch this film at our event in Warsaw, Poland. It's an interesting story of die-hard Bitcoiners fighting for sound money for their homeland.
- **DIRTY COIN**, the title we're all waiting for — after numerous awards for *STRANDED: A DIRTY COIN SHORT*, Alana Mediavilla is ready to showcase her entire film. Live from Warsaw, with an official screening and an unforgettable pre-premiere party, it's another compelling reason to join us in April at block 840,000.
