# Promoting Bitcoin: A Space That Most Wouldn't Expect

*The original piece, part one, was published in [European Bitcoiners](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/pow-and-bitcoin-themed-advertising-part-one/). Below is an extended version with more videos, including an excerpt from an article by AZA_21m.*

"My two sats on earning attention instead of buying it. When ads mine hearts and minds, not your data.

It's often said that 'Bitcoin doesn't need marketing,' and the internet proves it daily. Instead of typical 'sell-me-anything' ads, the space thrives on organic memes and threads: a grassroots testament to Bitcoin's ethos. This isn't the artificial promotion of a manufactured desire, where ads dictate specific behaviors. Bitcoiners don't just reject Proof-of-Hype, so typical in a fiat-driven world; they replace it with Proof-of-Work: value emerging not from coercion, but from consensus, meaning what the community validates as genuine and meaningful."

## Why Bring This Up?

First, because Bitcoiners weaponize even simple ideas better than entire Wall Street-hired PR firms. Every "Bitcoin doesn't need marketing" or "Thanks, Fed, for the free promo" is a clever, almost subversive way of raising topics worth noticing. Exposing fiat's failures often speaks louder than directly evangelizing how sound Bitcoin is, and how well it solves problems that keep many non-coiners awake at night.

Second, credit where it's due: Bitcoin-only projects and individuals actively spreading the word, hard money, real solutions, and the culture itself. This isn't "marketing" in the traditional, pushy, profit-at-all-costs sense. Instead, it's built on:

- Information: no bullshit, just facts
- Education: orange-pilling, not upselling
- Community: value, instead of dry PR
- Creativity: proof-of-mind, not algo-bait
- Experiences: how-to solutions, not showing off

Earning money isn't evil; it's necessary for these projects to survive. What matters is the intention. No one should apologize for creating Bitcoin-themed ads, so long as they carry meaning instead of hype.
