# Welcome to the month of All-Time Highs! Let’s dive into the latest from the world of Bitcoin + independent cinema, with the overall spirits running high too.

*November 29, 2024*

## Catch Up: A Peek From a Few Weeks Ago

**#01:** At the beginning this month, Jessica Solce gave her latest film to the public. [FORGING A COUNTRY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CvzSx5xfNIo) is now available online, completely free to watch. What are you waiting for?

**— TRAILER —**

**#02:** For private rentals or group screenings, check out the award-winning production DIRTY COIN by Alana Mediavilla, which debunks myths about Bitcoin mining. [Here’s one of the first platforms](https://kinema.com/films/dirty-coin-the-bitcoin-mining-documentary-46arp1) to offer multiple viewing options.

**#03:** [Bitcoin Shooter](https://x.com/BitcoinShooter)’s latest production, NO MORE INFLATION, is now LIVE! Teased at various conferences earlier this year, the film was finalized just a few days ago and is now [available to watch online](https://nomoreinflation.com/) for a small fee ($9.99).

**— TRAILER —**

## BFF-Buzz: What's Hot and Happening These Days?

**#01:** [Max De Marco](https://x.com/itsmaxdemarco), known for cool films on sovereign living, travel, Nostr, and Bitcoin, has launched an interesting show, PROOF OF HEALTH. The first few episodes are available in both audio and video formats. Head over to [YouTube and check them out](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLbku38jgMk8W52DgDhlCIHScTawPIlX14)!

**#02:** The mini-grant from [Geyser Fund](https://geyser.fund/grants/12), created in partnership with the [Orange Pill App](https://x.com/orangepillapp), is freshly over. The initiative provided funding to support the production of five new films. Who knows? Some of them might be ready to premiere at [#BFF25](https://x.com/search?q=%23BFF25&src=typed_query).

**#03:** Speaking of 2025 and the next edition of our festival, the dates are locked in. Mark your calendars: [May 22-25, Warsaw, Poland](https://bitcoinfilmfest.com/bff25/). More details will be announced soon. Stay tuned!

## Bitcoin Cinema: Reels to Keep an Eye On

📽️ **13 STEPS TO BECOME AN INTERSTELLAR SPECIES**

A short film (11:48) by Bit Intelligence, which is an interesting adaptation of the article by Alex Svetski. [The Great Filter Theory and the Fermi Paradox](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3i2pXdOAerc)…

📽️ **BITCOIN BASH AND CORRUPTED CASH**

Who said cartoons are only for kids? The latest episode of [Tuttle Twins](https://www.angel.com/shows/tuttle-twins), Season 3 that explores bitcoin vs crypto, inflation, and CBDCs — is sure a great option for all generations.

📽️ **FRANKENSKIES**

An [80-minute documentary](https://www.youtube.com/@frankenskies3681/videos) about the Solar Geoengineering/Chemtrail agenda. *“He who controls the weather, controls the world.”*
