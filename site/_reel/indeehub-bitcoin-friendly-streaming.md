# INDEEHUB STUDIO

***“to liberate the art of filmmaking”***

**Imagine: Netflix x IMDB, built on Bitcoin. It’s already here, and it’s called [IndeeHub](https://x.com/indeehub). We’re excited to announce one of our partners, IndeeHub Studio, a new VOD platform that aims to empower independent filmmakers and offer viewers a simple way to support their favorite content with Bitcoin LN. It is the first solution on the market to enable streaming sats for watched content and to distribute collected funds among the people involved in a given film.**

**🧡 Not only does IndeeHub shift the power dynamics in the current film industry by allowing creators to monetize their content, but it also brings them closer to fans.**

**For cinema fans, it offers an opportunity to support filmmakers whose work they admire while promising broader access to quality content due to increased production.**

**As a platform that offers on-demand streaming, it gives its users the freedom to decide when and how they spend their funds — whether it’s paying because they enjoy the content or opting not to pay if it doesn’t meet their expectations. This flexibility empowers viewers to support films they value and encourages the creation of more high-quality content in the future.**

**For us, the heart of Bitcoin Cinema, IndeeHub’s solution unveils a world of new opportunities. It helps us promote films and creators we collaborate with to a broader audience, as well as to please our followers, offering them access to numerous great videos all in one spot. While this is just the beginning, we’re happy to embark on this journey — hand in hand with IndeeHub, all talented independent creators, and movie buffs who seek narratives beyond mainstream platforms.**

**Let’s also hear directkly from Zack, the founder of IndeeHub, about his perspective on our collaboration.**

🎙️ **BFF: Why did you decide to collaborate with Bitcoin FilmFest?**

💬 **Zack:** *It’s mostly because we share the same fundamental objectives. Our goal is to create a world where truth and beauty can flourish.*

🎙️ **BFF: What motivated you to build a ‘Netflix killer’? (as someone on one of the Alby Community Calls referred to IndeeHub)…**

💬 **Zack:** *Early in my career, I sought access to a fair and open market for my work, but such a platform didn’t exist. While Netflix initiated an incredible innovation in the exhibition of cinema, I believe the industry requires a revolution in its business model. There’s a pressing need to refocus on creators and viewers. Unfortunately, the relationship* *between audience and artist has become increasingly opaque, which I believe has led to a decline in storytelling quality over the past 10-15 years.*

🎙️ **BFF: Why could IndeeHub be considered a breakthrough in the industry?**

💬 **Zack:** *I believe that the breakthrough of IndeeHub lies in its approach to filmmaking business. Our aim is simple: to re-establish a direct, energetic link between the audience and the artist. When you watch a movie on our platform, the artists get paid. If you’re not watching, they’re not earning. This system allows filmmakers to take risks in a way that the current market structure discourages. By cutting out the middleman, we enable direct interaction between creators and viewers, providing an experience that’s lacking on many other platforms.*

🎙️ **BFF: What kind of movies can users expect on IndeeHub?**

💬 **Zack:** *We’re still in the early stages, but we already have a variety of compelling titles available. In fact, some films screened at Bitcoin FilmFest last year are already accessible on our platform. Over time, we’ll continue to add more, potentially including those that premiered at BFF24. There’s still plenty of work ahead of us, but we’re planning to add new content as often as possible — from short to feature-length productions*

🎙️ **BFF:  Thanks, Zack. Once again, congrats on launching such an important tool! We’re excited to be a part of it. All the best for the future!**

💬 **Zack:** *Likewise! It’s always great to talk with like-minded people. As for IndeeHub—simply create your account and enjoy, everyone!*

IndeHub Studio already offers a wide range of interesting films, along with series, music, and visual content. And as you can tell from the interview above, there’s even more to come.

The best way to discover what we offer is to visit the platform: **[indeehub.studio](https://indeehub.studio/)**; as a cinema-fan and as a creator.
