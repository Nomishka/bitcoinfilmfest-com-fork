*June 19, 2024*

## Catch Up: A Peek From a Few Weeks Ago.

![Bitcoin Cinema Digest - Topic Spotlight]({{ '/assets/images/newsletters/summer-2024/spotlight.png' | relative_url }} "spotlight _117_web icon-dark blue_BFF")

**#01:** After the successful premiere of DIRTY COIN in Warsaw during BFF24, additional screenings have been held in Europe and beyond. We recently supported one of those in Berlin (as a part of [#TheBitcoinMeetup](https://x.com/hashtag/TheBitcoinMeetup)).

Alongside [DIRTY COIN](https://x.com/DirtyCoinDoc), several trailers of upcoming titles scheduled for this year were screened as well. Results? Full room and fantastic feedback! 📷 Catch [a glimpse on X](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1799378436680749352).

**#02:** In partnership with [Fedi](https://x.com/fedibtc) + [Bitcoin News](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom), we’ve just wrapped up the [#LightningLuminary](https://x.com/search?q=%23LightningLuminary&src=typed_query&f=live) Video Contest.

**With $1500 in sats awarded across 3 distinct categories, the winners are:**

📽️ Storyteller ($700): [Mr. Black](https://cze0h.r.sp1-brevo.net/mk/cl/f/sh/SMK1E8tHeGF9IwZX59RttHcWiy2h/l_OTsLli-eUY)

🦾 Innovation ($500): [Elie Rosinski](https://cze0h.r.sp1-brevo.net/mk/cl/f/sh/SMK1E8tHeGM0l5OUGJbNz6vGhGcx/Elf6uRrhTWSG)

🙌 Community ($150 each): [Isabella](https://cze0h.r.sp1-brevo.net/mk/cl/f/sh/SMK1E8tHeGSsDEDRRTks4wE0fZDD/4_RDmrr9bcWw) + [Wahyu Gunawan](https://cze0h.r.sp1-brevo.net/mk/cl/f/sh/SMK1E8tHeGZjfN2OcduMAlWkdrnT/iebWZczKupvG)

**Take a look at their entries by checking links above, and give them well-deserved congrats!**

**#03:** Every now and then, questions arise about whether Binema should be solely about Bitcoin. A while back, we started a thread about mainstream movies that deserve a place in this space. One of [our team members](https://cze0h.r.sp1-brevo.net/mk/cl/f/sh/SMK1E8tHeGgb7VrLno3qGapUcANj/kT4uY_Cy8k89) wrote a an interesting article about it for [Love Is Bitcoin](https://cze0h.r.sp1-brevo.net/mk/cl/f/sh/SMK1E8tHeGnSZegIyyDKMQ8EaSxz/i6CYRnG5zyGF). If you have a second, ake a look 📰 Feel free to share it on socials. We’re curious to hear what you think 👇

## Bitcoin Cinema: Reels to Keep an Eye On

![Bitcoin Cinema Digest, films]({{ '/assets/images/newsletters/summer-2024/films.png' | relative_url }} "video-player _117_web icon")

📽️ **21 VOICES**

A new series of short documentaries from Swan Studio and Bitcoin News, produced by [@luke\_broyles](https://x.com/luke_broyles) + [@bevan\_waite](https://x.com/bevan_waite) is coming up in less than a week. [Check it out here](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoB1eZWSVHVYnXEMptb3ZHUpgWWp-KfZI).

📽️ **DARE TO DREAM**

Reminder of a good film that was released last year: [96 minute feature documentary](https://x.com/bitcoin_films) by Graham Rittener. Touching, inspiring, thought-provoking. A must-see!

🎬 **PARALLEL SPACE**

Progress that needs your support: Documentary film by [@PIGSON\_M](https://x.com/PIGSON_M) about searching for freedom through parallel structures. Help its progress by support this project [on Geyser Fund](https://geyser.fund/project/parallelspace).

🎬 **Bitcoin RE₿EL**

One of the titles presented on the Community Stage at [#BFF24](https://x.com/search?q=%23BFF24&src=typed_query&f=live): “the story of brothers and brothers from the heart”. If you want to see its premiere next year, consider sending some SATS to their campaign [on Geyser](https://geyser.fund/project/rebel).
