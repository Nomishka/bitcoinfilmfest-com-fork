— **BFF BUZZ: Orange Lens on Culture** —

**#01:** The 3rd episode, in collaboration with [Juan Cienfuegos](https://x.com/TheJuanSC) (BitCorner Podcast), [went out in March](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSt-ycDdgSo). Special guest: [Martin Piga](https://x.com/PIGSON_M), the brain behind **PARALLEL SPACES** (a new film, in progress). Spoiler: If you join us this May at [Bitcoin FilmFest 2025](https://bitcoinfilmfest.com/bff25/), you’ll learn more about his production and meet both guys in person.

**#02:** 3 new films in making, grassroots adoption in different countries: 1) **[BITCOIN RISING – THE PATH TO FREEDOM IN SOUTH AFRICA](https://x.com/BitcoinEkasi/status/1909900442584175049)** by Jonas Nilsson. 2) **[THE BULGARIAN JOURNEY](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/1903132701005107292)** by Kyle Huber and nackoo2000. 3) **[INSIDE JAPAN’S BITCOIN REVOLUTION](https://x.com/kinetic_finance/status/1906882173472489925)** by Julian Figueroa. Follow them on socials, and help spread the word.

**#03:** **DIRTY COIN**, winner of the 2024 Golden Rabbits (Best Movie [#BFF24](https://x.com/hashtag/BFF24)), is heading to Cannes. [Reshare the news](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1913600655048011994) and contact Alana to support her work.

**#04:** Geyser Fund released a new feature to amplify Bitcoin projects driving change from the ground up. We’re one of the 1st partners to try it out. [Geyser Promotions Network](https://x.com/geyserfund/status/1912495558024999094). More to come soon. Stay tuned.

**#05:** From April 7th, you can already enjoy the first-ever Bitcoin fiction podcast. “**[Mysteries of the Bitcoin Citadel](https://x.com/paperstreet_inc)**”. Engaging narratives brought to life through audio storytelling. Who knows when it might be headed for a cinematic debut…

— **Bitcoin Cinema: Reels to Keep an Eye On** —

🎞️ **GET ON THE MISSION:** [Short film documenting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=125lp2jTzeM) how the [Bitcoin Veterans](https://x.com/BitcoinVeterans) took their movement to the next level. Discussions on financial and personal sovereignty, plus an invitation to the upcoming event in [Alaska](https://bitcoinalaska.org).

🎞️ **CYPHERPUNKS WRITE CODE:** A great topic to revisit through a four-part [documentary series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBuns9Evn1w-T2RwqMhUnTZbTTe-M-g42), presenting the epic movement that played a key role in the creation of Bitcoin, WikiLeaks, and more.

🎞️ **THE NEXT PARADIGM IN MEDICINE:** One of the recent videos from [Ioni Appelberg](https://x.com/IoniAppelberg). “*Is this the start of a future without age-related diseases?*” [Tune in to find out](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZ3nXx9V55M).

🎞️ **DO YOU HAVE A LIFEBOAT:** Another [great short](https://rumble.com/v6rsegb-do-you-have-a-lifeboat.html) from [₿urn The Fed](https://x.com/BurnTheFed). This time exposing hard truths about the Federal Reserve and how current economic policies are pushing people deeper into inflation.

🎥 **THE LEGEND OF LANDI (in the making):** One of the titles from the [pitch contest during BFF24](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1784576551066939857). It’s made quite a progress, which, by the way, you’ll get to experience in May at [#BFF25](https://x.com/hashtag/BFF25), but it still needs your support. Head over to [Geyser Fund](https://geyser.fund/project/legendoflandi?hero=bitcoinfilmfest) and consider contributing to its creation.
