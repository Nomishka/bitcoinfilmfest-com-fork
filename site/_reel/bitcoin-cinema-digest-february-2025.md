# Your cinematic wrap-up of last month: 2 announcements, 4 podcasts, 5 updates, 7 movie titles to enjoy!

*February 26, 2025*

**🧡 ANNOUNCEMENT 🐇**

Early bird tickets for Bitcoin FilmFest 2025 are available until March.

🎟️ Book your spot now with 50% off.

See you in [Warsaw at BFF25](https://bitcoinfilmfest.com/bff25/)!

— **BFF BUZZ: Orange Lens on Culture —**

**#01:** Alana Mediavilla’s **[DIRTY COIN](https://kinema.com/films/dirty-coin-the-bitcoin-mining-documentary-46arp1)** has been nominated for Best Feature-Length Documentary at the [Barcelona Indie Awards](https://barcelonaindieawards.com/nominee-season-2025/).

**#02:** We teamed up with [SatsNFacts](https://x.com/satsandfacts) (**the first Bitcoin Technical Unconference in Asia**) to host [a mini-cinematic experience](https://x.com/bitcoinfilmfest/status/1889727343943430303), featuring 7 trailers and 10 short films.

**#03:** Our Mr. Rabbit sat down for a chilled chat with **BitcoinNews**. Read [the full interview here](https://bitcoinnews.com/interviews/bitcoin-news-interview-bitcoin-filmfest/).

**#04:** Channel 4 (UK) has started working on [a new documentary series](https://www.channel4.com/programmes/mystery-of-the-bitcoin-billionaire?) **SEEKING SATOSHI: THE MYSTERY BITCOIN CREATOR** to investigate the true identity of Satoshi Nakamoto.

**#05:** The second episode, in collaboration with [Juan Cienfuegos](https://x.com/TheJuanSC) (**BitCorner Podcast**), is out 👇 Special guest: [Max De Marco](https://x.com/itsmaxdemarco).

— **Bitcoin Cinema: Reels to Keep an Eye On** —

🎞️ **THE TRUST FALL: JULIAN ASSANGE:** ‘The story of a man who risked everything to bring the truth to light’ (2024, Kym Straton). The film is finally [available to watch online worldwide](https://filmsforchange.stream/catalog) ($9.95).

🎞️ **RULE BREAKERS:** A new [production from Angel Studios](https://www.angel.com/movies/rule-breakers), coming out in March. Roya Mahboob and her heroic story — a strong Bitcoin advocate promoting tech and financial freedom for women in Afghanistan. Watch its [trailer here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CW_P4zT6i9A).

🎞️ **THE GREATEST HEIST IN HISTORY:** Inspiring animation by [Bevan Waite](https://x.com/bevan_waite), based on the article by Tomer Strolight. ‘How does it feel to owe someone money before you’re even born?’ [Watch it here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=52T543jP_gE).

🎞️ **HOTEL BITCOIN:** A comedy unlike any other. Parties, love, crime, a healthy dose of madness, and a pinch of education… Available in Spanish with English subtitles. [Where to watch](https://www.justwatch.com/us/movie/hotel-bitcoin).

🎥 **BITCOIN MOVIE OR ZERO WASTE GIRL (work in progress):** A comedy movie about a street artist who accidentally tosses out a laptop with 100 BTC inside. Support its efforts via [Geyser Fund](https://geyser.fund/project/bitcoinmovie?hero=03b15705cbd6) (no amount is small).

📣 **ANNOUNCEMENT** 🔔

FINANCIAL FALLOUT: The next anthology from [21 Futures](https://21futures.com/) is coming out on March 14th. Gripping Bitcoin-freedom-themed fiction stories. Great reads, and who knows, maybe some exciting shorts to watch later on.
