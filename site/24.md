      <p class="bff24-section-label">01 · the edition</p>
      <h2>Films at the edge of the halving.</h2>
      <div class="bff24-story-grid">
        <div>
          <p class="bff24-lead-copy">BFF’24 was the film-festival component of the wider <strong>European Halving Party</strong>, whose programme ran from 18–21 April 2024. The festival itself ran from 19–21 April at Kinoteka, Warsaw.</p>
          <p>The setting mattered: a Bitcoin cinema gathering in the Palace of Culture and Science, a landmark of the city’s twentieth-century history. The public record describes a weekend built around screenings, conversations and the shared anticipation of the fourth Bitcoin halving at block 840,000.</p>
        </div>
        <figure class="bff24-story-image"><img src="{{ '/24/24-assets/bff24-banner.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Bitcoin FilmFest 2024 Warsaw banner artwork" width="1422" height="968"><figcaption>Recovered BFF’24 identity artwork</figcaption></figure>
      </div>
      <div class="bff24-fact-line"><span>19–21 April</span><span>Kinoteka</span><span>Warsaw, Poland</span><span>block 840,000</span></div>

  <section class="bff24-band bff24-band--paper" id="programme">
    <div class="bff24-inner">
      <p class="bff24-section-label">02 · programme shape</p>
      <h2>A festival, not just a screening list.</h2>
      <p class="bff24-band-lede">The recovered programme and post-event record support the shape of the weekend below. The old timetable is not reproduced as an authoritative running order.</p>
      <div class="bff24-programme-grid">
        <article><span class="bff24-number">01</span><h3>Screenings &amp; premieres</h3><p>Feature documentaries, shorts and independent films were shown at Kinoteka. The selection included five premieres and a broader set of public screenings.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff24-number">02</span><h3>Community Stage</h3><p>A public community programme ran at Kinoteka on 19 and 20 April, creating room for voices and conversations around the films.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff24-number">03</span><h3>Amondo mornings</h3><p>Amondo Breakfast and Amondo Workshops ran on 19 and 20 April. The archive also records Junior and Kids Cinema as part of the wider event’s family-facing programme.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff24-number">04</span><h3>ShortFest &amp; CartoonFest</h3><p>ShortFest was recorded at Kinoteka on 20 April, with <em>Bitcoiners</em>, <em>HODL</em>, <em>The Lekker Feeling</em> and <em>Peru</em>; CartoonFest and Kids Cinema formed part of the 21 April programme context, including <em>Adventures of Jonathan</em>, <em>The Maxis Club Show</em> and <em>Bitcoin &amp; Friends</em>.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff24-number">05</span><h3>Halving weekend</h3><p>The fourth Bitcoin halving completed during the Warsaw BFF’24 / European Halving Party programme. That countdown is the historical frame, not a live event prompt.</p></article>
        <article><span class="bff24-number">06</span><h3>Closing ceremony</h3><p>The weekend closed with a ceremony at Kinoteka, Golden Rabbit awards and an after-after party at Amondo in the recorded programme context.</p></article>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
